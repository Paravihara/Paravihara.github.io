## A quiet reading path

This site will gather talks, scripture translations, concept entries, and further reading. Each text will keep its original chapter structure while making related ideas easy to follow.

:::tip
Use the language picker in the header to switch between 中文 and English. The two languages share the same chapter structure, and unfinished translations can fall back to the default language.
:::

## Start here

- [Teachings](/en/talks/): talks arranged by series and chapter.
- [Scriptures](/en/sutras/): read translations with optional source text and notes.
- [Concepts](/en/concepts/): follow a term into related passages and teachings.
