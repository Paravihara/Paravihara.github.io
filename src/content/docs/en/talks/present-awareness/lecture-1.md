# Staying with the present · Lecture 1

> A sample teaching page. A finished page can include audio, a transcript, editorial notes, and chapter navigation.

## Begin with experience

Practice does not have to begin with a distant answer. Begin by seeing what is happening: how a thought appears, how a feeling changes, and how attention returns to the breath and body.

When we say “awareness,” we are not standing outside experience to judge it. We let experience become clear within careful attention. See also [mindfulness](../../../../concepts/mindfulness/).
